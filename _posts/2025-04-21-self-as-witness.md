A friend and I are swapping COVID stories. She's describing the many TikToks her and friends filmed in the purgatory between Lockdowns. "If we'd made them when we were 12," she groans, "I'd definitely find them cute by now".

Her off-hand comment sticks. On one level, it's as if she's managed to stray far enough from her pre-teen self to afford detachment, even fondness. Rumination ❌ reminiscence ✅. On another, this is a confession: Dredged up home videos make her cringe because she knows the girl in the frame - she's still that girl.

I think about this a lot. There are moments I find hard to revisit. Does that mean I still sit with the people I used to be? And given how cliche it is to feel beholden to a parliament of our past selves, perhaps we never really leave the table. Why should identity be this unbroken line - this thread of selfhood that hasn't frayed over the years? If the past action makes you cringe, do you own the memory, or does it own you? Why do you remain very much at one with the one who did the thing? My guess: her childhood feels 'cute' because my friend no longer expects her current self to answer for it. She's filed it away under a former identity, and in doing so stepped far enough back to reserve any kind of judgement.

I also suspect that _noticing_ matters here; That catching yourself 'cringe' marks a shift from 'self-as-subject' to 'self-as-witness'. This might be the mind's admission: "I'm not _fully_ rid of that yet." Not trapped, exactly - but not absolved either. There's a quiet dignity in occupying this liminal space. To echo Alaine De Botton, if you don't flinch at who you were a year ago, you surely haven't grown enough.

We spend our lives atop rickety bridges, from one persona to the next. When we cringe, we're suspended halfway - close enough to profess the missteps, too close to forgive them. Only once we reach terra firma can we examine the crossing  with grace and patience. I hope I'll read this with grace and patience - someday. For now, I'll admit, this stuff is **\_\_\_\_\_\_**.
